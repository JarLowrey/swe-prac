## Posting

Northwind Traders runs the marketplace that independent grocers use to buy from regional suppliers. Every order on Northwind moves money between three parties, and the Payments Platform team builds the systems that make that safe: the ledger, payouts to suppliers, and the integrations with card networks and banks.

We're hiring a Senior Backend Engineer to help us move from a batch-based payout system to near-real-time payouts.

**What you'll do**
- Design and build services in Go that handle payment authorization, ledgering and payouts.
- Own reliability for the services you build, including on-call, SLOs and incident follow-up.
- Lead projects across teams, from design doc to rollout.
- Mentor other engineers and raise the bar on code and design review.

**What we're looking for**
- 6+ years building backend systems, with production experience in Go, Java or a similar language.
- Experience with financial systems: ledgers, reconciliation, idempotency, or payment processors.
- Strong PostgreSQL skills and experience with event streaming (Kafka or similar).
- A track record of migrating critical systems without downtime.

**Nice to have**
- Experience with card networks or bank payout rails (ACH, RTP).
- Experience improving on-call health or observability for a team.

Northwind is an equal opportunity employer.
